OnFiNtY Gym Landing Page

A dynamic fitness gym landing page featuring animated particles, gradient effects, and motivational design for modern fitness establishments

Project Overview

Fitness Motivation Design

Dark theme with cyan and coral accents, featuring "Transform Your Body, Elevate Your Mind" messaging designed to inspire and motivate fitness enthusiasts.

Comprehensive Programs

Four main training programs: Strength Training, Cardio Blast, HIIT Training, and Mind & Body (yoga/meditation) with interactive booking functionality.

Animated Statistics

Dynamic counter animations displaying key metrics: 500+ members trained, 100% personalized plans, and 50+ weekly classes with scroll-triggered animations.

Key Components

Animated Particle Background

50 floating particles with cyan glow effects, random positioning, and continuous animation creating an energetic, dynamic atmosphere throughout the site.

Four Core Features Grid

Personal Training, Modern Equipment, Flexible Classes (50+ weekly), and Community features with glass morphism cards and hover animations.

Auto-sliding Testimonials

Three success stories from Sarah Johnson, Mike Rodriguez, and Emma Chen with automatic carousel functionality and smooth transitions.

Complete Contact Information

Footer with contact details (123 Fitness Street), operating hours, social media links, and comprehensive gym information layout.

Technical Implementation

Animated Counter System

Intersection Observer API with incremental counter animations triggered on scroll

Custom Cursor Glow Effect

Dynamic cursor tracking with radial gradient glow following mouse movement

Ripple Click Effects

Interactive button animations with expanding ripple effects on click events

Glass Morphism Design

Backdrop filters and transparent overlays creating depth and modern aesthetics

CSS Transform Animations

Card hover effects with translateY, scale transforms, and smooth transitions

Auto-sliding Carousel

JavaScript-powered testimonial slider with 5-second intervals and smooth transitions

Design & Development Details

Brand Identity: OnFiNtY Gym - Modern fitness center with "Transform Your Body, Elevate Your Mind" philosophy
Color Scheme: Dark theme (#0E0E1B primary, #1A1A2E secondary), cyan accent (#00D8FF), coral accent (#FF6F61)
Animations: Particle system, scroll animations, auto-slider, ripple effects, cursor tracking
Layout: Full-screen hero, responsive grid systems, glass morphism cards, gradient overlays
Skills Demonstrated: Fitness industry design, advanced animations, interactive UI elements, motivational messaging
Time taken to build: Approximately 19-24 hours
Pricing: $150 - $240 (based on animation complexity and interactive feature implementation)
